SmartRecord

A focused macOS recorder with SmartFocus click zoom, a built-in timeline editor with annotations, and H.264 MP4 export — all processed locally.

Version 1.10. Requires macOS with ScreenCaptureKit support.

Complete capture

Record the display, microphone, system audio, or any combination — start and stop with a global hotkey.

SmartFocus

Every click triggers a smooth punch-zoom on the region being explained, then eases back to full screen.

Edit & annotate

Trim and split the timeline, then add text, arrows, highlights, blur, and images before exporting H.264 MP4.

English

SmartRecord is a macOS screen recorder for tutorials, demos, and product walkthroughs. It captures screen video, microphone narration, and system audio while SmartFocus automatically zooms in on your clicks with a smooth punch-zoom. The built-in editor lets you trim and split the timeline and add text, arrows, highlights, blur, and image annotations that stay locked to the zoomed content, then export an H.264 MP4. Everything runs locally on your Mac.
